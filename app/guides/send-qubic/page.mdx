# Send QU

QU is the native unit on Qubic. A transfer goes to a 60-character Qubic identity, such as a saved contact or an identity you paste in.

<Callout title="Start small">
A signed transfer cannot be reversed from the wallet. Send a small test amount first, confirm it arrives, and only then send the rest.
</Callout>

## Before you send

- Unlock the Vault that holds the account you want to use.
- Select the correct Qubic identity. A Vault can hold several.
- Check the recipient identity carefully. Qubic identities are long, and one wrong character sends funds somewhere you do not control.
- Know the amount and enough about the remaining balance to spot a mistake.

The review screen shows the account, recipient, amount, and remaining balance before you sign.

## Send a transfer

1. Open your Vault and select the account you want to send from.
2. Start a transfer.
3. Choose a saved contact or enter the recipient identity.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Add a local memo if you want to recognize the payment later. Memos and notes stay in your wallet data.
6. Review the details, then sign.

Glyph tracks the transaction from broadcast through confirmation, expiry, or failure. The history view is searchable, and you can keep local notes and tags on activity.

## Send to multiple recipients

Glyph supports sending to multiple recipients in one reviewed flow. Build the list, review each recipient and amount, then sign. Check the full list before you approve it. A transfer that has already been signed cannot be edited or pulled back from Glyph.

## Receive QU

To receive QU, share one of these from the account:

- the Qubic identity itself
- a QR code for the identity
- a payment link

The sender still needs the correct identity or link. Confirm that the destination account matches the wallet you control before you ask someone to send funds.

## Track the transaction

A transfer moves through several states. Glyph shows the current state so you can tell the difference between a transaction that is still being processed and one that has failed or expired.

If a transfer looks stuck, first check:

- the transaction state in Glyph
- the recipient identity and amount you signed
- whether the wallet is using the Qubic network or a custom RPC endpoint
- the [user guide](https://github.com/glyphq/wallet/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md) for the current limitations

## Reviewing app requests

Glyph can also receive supported deep links from connected Qubic apps. A transfer request from an app is shown in the wallet with its account, action, amount, and details. Review it the same way you review a transfer you started yourself, and decline anything you do not recognize.

Connected apps and their permissions live under **Settings → Connected apps**. You can remove access there.
